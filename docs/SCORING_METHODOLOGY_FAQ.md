# Scoring methodology and help FAQ

Public URL: `/scoring-methodology`, linked from `/how-it-works`.
This route draws deterministic criterion weights directly from `app.scoring.engine.WEIGHTS`;
its descriptions explain uncertainty, search metadata vs document analysis,
company-profile changes, source coverage and human decision review.

It **does not** promise bid award, official RNP risk verification, court-history
signals, complete source coverage or automatically converted budgets.
More comprehensive multilingual knowledge-base authoring and support search
remain planned. Do not use a fit score as a win probability.
